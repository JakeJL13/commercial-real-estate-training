# CRE Chart of Accounts (Simplified)

## Revenue

- **Base Rental Income** — contractual base rent from all tenants, gross of abatements
- **CAM Recoveries** — tenant reimbursements for common area maintenance, taxes, insurance
- **Parking Income** — monthly and transient parking revenue
- **Late Fees & Other** — miscellaneous income, late fees, storage, antenna
- **Vacancy Loss** — contra-revenue for unleased space (should be negative)

## Operating Expenses

### Fixed
- **Real Estate Taxes** — property tax expense
- **Property Insurance** — building and liability insurance premiums
- **Property Management Fee** — management company fees, typically 3% of EGI

### Variable / Controllable
- **Utilities** — electric, water, gas, sewer, trash
- **Janitorial** — cleaning contracts and supplies
- **Repairs & Maintenance** — non-capital maintenance
- **Landscaping** — grounds and exterior maintenance
- **Security** — security guard contracts and monitoring
- **Marketing** — leasing marketing, signage, brochures
- **Administrative & Other** — office supplies, professional fees, other

## Below the NOI Line

- **Capital Expenditures (CapEx)** — building system replacements, major roof, elevator
- **Tenant Improvements (TI)** — build-out allowances for new or renewing tenants
- **Leasing Commissions (LC)** — broker commissions on new leases and renewals
- **Debt Service** — interest and principal (not in this model)

## Key Metrics Referenced

- **Effective Gross Income (EGI)** = Base Rental Income + CAM Recoveries + Parking + Other + Vacancy Loss
- **Net Operating Income (NOI)** = EGI − Total Operating Expenses
- **Operating Expense Ratio** = Total OpEx / EGI
- **NOI Margin** = NOI / EGI
- **NOI PSF** = NOI / Rentable SF
- **Cash Flow After CapEx** = NOI − CapEx − TI − LC
